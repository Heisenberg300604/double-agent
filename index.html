    DOUBLE AGENT

    One target. One hunter. A station full of strangers.

    Double Agent is played with a mouse and keyboard. Have a look around, then come back on a computer to play.

    What is Double Agent?

    Everyone is a stranger.

    8 assassins hide among 112 commuters in near-identical charcoal suits. Act like a commuter and nobody looks twice.

    Hunt your target.

    Your dossier describes one person: tie colour, hat, glasses, hair. Find them in the crowd. Q scans for their direction.

    Someone is hunting you.

    Every spy hunts the next in a ring. Kill your target and you inherit theirs. Your hunter is out there, watching for a tell.

    Blend in or be found.

    Running, aiming and the tracks raise suspicion in whoever sees you; sitting and reading (G) cool it. A hunter whose suspicion of you passes 60 sees you glow red.
